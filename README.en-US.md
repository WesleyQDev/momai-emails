# MomAI Emails

Manage your inboxes and send email directly through MomAI using voice or text commands. Compatible with Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud and custom servers.

## Your organized inbox at your fingertips

With MomAI Emails, you do not need to interrupt what you are doing on your computer to check important messages or reply to urgent contacts. Everything can be done quickly and conversationally with your assistant.

### Key Features

- **Multiple Connected Accounts**: Add and switch between Gmail, Outlook, Hotmail, Yahoo, iCloud or work accounts in one place.
- **Real-Time Alerts**: Receive desktop and sidebar notifications as soon as new messages arrive, so you never miss anything important.
- **Automatic Sorting**: Your email is clearly organized into categories such as Primary, Promotions, Updates and Social to avoid overload.
- **Voice Reading and Summaries**: MomAI reads message content to you and summarizes long threads in seconds.
- **Quick Replies**: Dictate or type your replies and send them instantly without opening the browser.
- **Local Security and Encryption**: Your credentials are stored with the built-in encryption of your own computer. No password is sent to third-party servers.

## What you can ask MomAI

- "MomAI, do I have any new email in my inbox?"
- "Read the latest email I received from the work team."
- "Send an email to contact@company.com saying the report is already approved."
- "Reply to the latest email confirming the meeting for tomorrow at 2pm."
- "Mark all promotional emails as read."

## How to Set Up

1. Open MomAI and click the Emails icon in the sidebar.
2. Click the connect account button and select your provider (such as Gmail or Outlook).
3. Enter your email address and the app password generated in your account.
4. Done! Your messages will sync and you can use them from the panel or ask the assistant directly.
